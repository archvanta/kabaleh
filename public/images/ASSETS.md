# Asset provenance

`tabletop.webp`, `still-life.webp`, `garden.webp`, and `window.webp` are original AI-generated decorative/sample images created for this project. They depict no personal photographs. WebP copies preserve the full compositions without cropping. Replace demonstration photos through `content/letter.json`.

The public demonstration intentionally uses `image: null` for personal photographs. The 21 supplied originals, including Belfi and the handmade gift, are verified and retained only in `.private/originals/`; the opt-in local content file selects them. They are never copied to `public/` or committed. The public interface displays clearly labeled empty paper mounts. Do not generate substitutes or interpret/transcribe handwriting. Preserve originals byte for byte when adding them; use their natural aspect ratio without filters, cropping, or retouching.

Botanical SVG drawings, character illustrations, paper textures and the thread path are original component artwork. The characters are an independent interpretation and are labeled accordingly; they are not a reproduction of the physical gift.

The current user-selected font is Mahboubeh Mehravar, supplied locally and excluded from Git redistribution. Ownership documents remain private. Vazirmatn is the fallback and is distributed by `@fontsource/vazirmatn` under SIL OFL-1.1. Its license is included in the package. GSAP is distributed under its standard license; see its package LICENSE. Lucide icons are ISC licensed. No artwork was copied from the reference websites.
