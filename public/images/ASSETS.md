# Asset provenance

The 21 user-provided original photographs are now explicitly authorized for this website's public GitHub deployment. They are preserved byte for byte in `images/originals/` in their original 14 source groups. Their archive checksums are recorded in `content/photo-manifest.json`. No faces, handwriting, colors, backgrounds or image proportions were altered. The full originals remain accessible through the gallery.

`tabletop.webp`, `still-life.webp`, `garden.webp` and `window.webp` are original AI-generated decorative/sample artwork from the initial design phase. The current personal narrative uses only the provided original photographs. The tabletop is a decorative background and is separate from all personal photos.

The eight envelope frames, the paper grain, the pocket states, the flower note and the two-figure ink ornament in `stationery/` were generated for this revision. The ink ornament takes visual inspiration from the photographed handwritten gift. The continuous scroll path and botanical SVGs are original component artwork. The actual handwritten gift is independently preserved as its unchanged photograph. Model reconstructions are separate from reference photographs and do not claim scan accuracy.

The selected Mahboubeh Mehravar webfont is supplied for the website via the asset branch and checked by SHA-256. Owner documents stay outside public files. The fallback Vazirmatn is OFL-1.1. Lucide retains its package license.
